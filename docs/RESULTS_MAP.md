## Statistical conventions retained

- N-back brain–severity and brain–behavior analyses retain the recorded `Behavioral_Available` flag. They do not recreate it from accuracy. Imaging before this flag has 88 N-back participants; the primary eligible sample has 74.
- Age-adjusted partial Spearman removes ranked age from both ranked variables. Figure 3 annotations lead with this estimate; raw observations and descriptive fit lines remain plotted.
- Bayes factors are Gaussian rank-regression JZS mixture calculations with nuisance age/intercept controlled and primary scale r=1. They are within-task tests, not ratios or direct comparisons between tasks.
- Direct study k-means calls use the supplied `kmeans_Matlab.m` to avoid CANlab name shadowing.
- Resilient patients and Severe Patients are assigned by ordered k=2 NMDAS centers, separately within each task's available patient sample.
- The cold-pain plot omits three pairs from display using the recorded 3-scaled-MAD rule. Data, effect size, statistics, unfiltered plot and audit retain all 91 pairs.
- Figure 4A's GDF15–NMDAS panel uses Spearman in the original source. The script also exports Pearson as a separately labeled check. The reproduced Spearman value is .75816; the manuscript reports .75. No manuscript edits were made during packaging.

## Historical scripts

`legacy_analysis` and `legacy_imaging` preserve selected source workflows for provenance. They include old paths, earlier variable names, dependencies and superseded display logic. Home-directory prefixes and literal participant IDs are redacted. They are not called by `RUN_ALL`, are not advertised as runnable without configuration, and should not override the current portable entry points. The 100-repetition source SVM-fitting loop is in `StandardAnalysis_Mito2nd.m`.
